**Purpose:** Help LushProtein decide *who* to treat differently, *how much* they can afford to give (without % discounts), and *how* to keep current high-value customers happy.

| Theme | Founder intent |
|-------|----------------|
| **Retention > acquisition** | Focus spend on keeping existing customers, not random promos |

## The core problem

**67.6% of customers buy once and never return.** Profit margin is highly concentrated:

| Segment | Customers | Avg profit margin | Share of pool profit margin |
|---------|-----------|-------------------|----------------------------|
| **PM D1** (top 20%) | 858 | **S$230** | **57.7%** |
| PM D5 (bottom 20%) | 858 | S$9 | 2.2% |
| **`is_top_both`** (VIP core) | **500** | **S$265** | 11.7% of pool |

A T5 one-and-done customer (S$9 profit margin) should not receive the same treatment as a T1 VIP (S$258 profit margin, 4.1 orders, 54% subscribed). Blanket 10% site-wide discounts cost **S$14K GP/yr** if they hit profit D1 customers — with no retention gain.

## Profit margin — definition (founder-scoped)

```
Profit Margin = Net Revenue − COGS
```

Discounts are already netted in Shopify revenue. Variable fulfilment and refunds are excluded (no reliable per-order data).

**Implementation (hybrid COGS):**

| Line type | Calculation |
|-----------|-------------|
| COGS known (74.7% of revenue) | `line_revenue − quantity × unit_cost` |
| COGS missing | `line_revenue × 40%` proxy |

**Customer profit margin** = SUM(line gross profit) → column `true_gross_profit` in `decile_customer_table.csv`

**Label for decks:** *Profit margin (hybrid COGS coverage)* — not audited net profit.

## Incentive budget framework — how much can LP give?

**Founder question:** *"If we give a customer an incentive, how much can we afford?"*

```
Maximum retention investment = X% × Customer Profit Margin
```

LP sets X% by tier. This is a **decision framework**, not a mandate to discount.

### By profit margin decile (pool average)

| PM Decile | Avg profit margin | **5% budget** | **10% budget** |
|-----------|-------------------|---------------|----------------|
| **D1** | S$230 | **S$11.50** | **S$23.01** |
| D2 | S$81 | S$4.06 | S$8.12 |
| D3 | S$49 | S$2.43 | S$4.86 |
| D4 | S$30 | S$1.48 | S$2.97 |
| D5 | S$9 | S$0.44 | S$0.89 |

### By treatment tier (what to actually spend)

| Tier | Avg 5% | Avg 10% | Appropriate incentives (NOT discounts) |
|------|--------|---------|----------------------------------------|
| **T1** | **S$12.91** | **S$25.82** | HYROX tickets, massage vouchers, VIP shaker, event invite |
| **T2** | S$5.85 | S$11.69 | Merch bundle, Subscribe & Save offer, category sample pack |
| **T3** | S$2.06 | S$4.12 | Cross-category sachet (COGS ~S$2–4) |
| **T4** | S$2.54 | S$5.08 | Single-serve insert (cap at 5% profit margin) |
| **T5** | S$0.44 | S$0.89 | Email only |

**Rule:** Never spend more than the **incremental profit margin** you expect from the action.

## What to give — incentive menu (NO % discounts)

| Tier | Give | Do NOT |
|------|------|--------|
| **T1** | Partner rewards (HYROX, events), early launches, exclusive shaker/merch, personal thank-you | Site-wide % off, mass promo codes |
| **T2** | Subscribe & Save, Clear+Lean bundle, shaker gift, category sample | Blanket 10–15% off |
| **T3** | Cross-category sachet, 2nd-order merch insert | Premium discounting |
| **T4** | Onboarding series, optional 1 sachet (≤S$2.54), education | Treat as loyal VIP |
| **T5** | Automated win-back (recent only) | Product gifts, manual outreach |

**Founder confirmation:** Merch and shakers work well for branding. Random discount promotions do not align with retention strategy.

## Keeping current customers happy — category ladder connection

59% of the pool has bought **only one category** (avg profit margin S$59, 17% repeat). Moving to 3 categories lifts profit margin to **S$144** and repeat to **55%**.

| Categories | Customers | % pool | Avg profit margin | Repeat rate |
|------------|-----------|--------|-------------------|-------------|
| 1 | 2,514 | 59% | S$59 | 17% |
| 2 | 1,216 | 28% | S$79 | 30% |
| 3 | 410 | 10% | **S$144** | **55%** |
| 4+ | 150 | 3% | S$249 | 82% |

**MBA evidence (PM D1 buyers):** Clear↔Lean co-purchase 53–65% — cross-sell is the retention lever, not deeper discounts.

T3/T4 customers get **category discovery** incentives (sachets, samples). T1/T2 get **loyalty experiences** (merch, events).
